Competition Structure

Tournament Format

Each age category comprises 10 teams divided into Group A and Group B for group stage play, followed by Cup and Plate knockout rounds.

Group Stage: 10 teams per category split into Group A and Group B.
Semifinal Cup: Top 2 teams from each group advance to the Cup semifinals.
Semifinal Plate: 3rd and 4th place from each group advance to the Plate semifinals.
Classification: 5th place teams play for 9th and 10th position ranking.
Match Duration: 15 minutes continuous playing time (1 half per match).
Trophies & Medals

Awards & Recognition

Every participant is celebrated, with dedicated trophies, medals, and e-certificates awarded across Cup and Plate brackets.

Cup Winners (1st–4th): Trophy + Medals + E-Certificates for Champion, Runner-Up, 3rd & 4th.
Plate Champion: Trophy + Medals + E-Certificate.
Plate Runners-Up & Placement (6th–10th): Medals + E-Certificates.
All Players: Every registered player receives a participation medal.
